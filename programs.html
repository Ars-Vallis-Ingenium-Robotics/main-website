Programs

Student engineering from idea to demonstration.

AVI programs teach students how to build, test, explain, fund, and improve technical projects. MATE ROV is a major pathway, not the only reason AVI exists.

What AVI supports

A complete build-season ecosystem.

⚙

Build sessions

Students prototype structures, controls, waterproof systems, tooling, and repair procedures while learning safe shop habits.

⌁

MATE ROV pathway

AVI supports the MATE ROV competition through Sequoia Marine Solutions: mission practice, documentation, presentations, and operations.

Visit MATE ROV ↗
▣

Technical communication

Students learn to explain tradeoffs, document failures, prepare sponsor materials, and present technical work to public audiences.

✦

Leadership and operations

AVI gives students room to practice team leadership, scheduling, purchasing discipline, fundraising, and volunteer coordination.

≈

Testing and iteration

Pool tests, checklists, and post-test fixes teach students that engineering is revision under pressure. Useful. Humbling. Necessary.

◇

Future robotics pathways

The long-term goal is a flexible organization that can support more than one robotics project as students and mentors step forward.
