Valley ingenuity, built in public.

Ars Vallis Ingenium Robotics, known as AVI Robotics, is a Fresno-centered 501(c)(3) nonprofit giving Central Valley students a serious place to build, test, present, volunteer, and lead. The name means roughly the skill and ingenuity of the valley: Ars is art or skill, Vallis is valley, and Ingenium is ingenuity or talent.

More than one competition

MATE is part of AVI, not the whole map.

AVI supports the full student engineering loop: design reviews, fabrication, controls, software, documentation, fundraising, presentations, outreach, volunteer service, and leadership.

Sequoia Marine Solutions has its own page as AVI’s MATE ROV competition identity. The larger organization is broader: a permanent robotics home for Fresno State students and the Central Valley community.

Current focus

AVI grew from real competition experience and now supports student builders, community service, and regional STEM events.
