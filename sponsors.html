Sponsors and donations

Fuel the next student-built machine.

Ars Vallis Ingenium Robotics is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. EIN: 39-4775163. Sponsorship helps Central Valley students get parts, tools, testing access, registration, travel support, and outreach materials.

Why support AVI

Local students need local backing.

AVI gives students a serious engineering environment without pretending resources appear from nowhere. Sponsors make the build season possible and help students represent Fresno State and the Central Valley with better tools, safer systems, and stronger preparation.
