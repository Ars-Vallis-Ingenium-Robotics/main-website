## hero

Nobody needs to know how to code, wire or CAD before joining. Workshops teach the basics every week, and Blank Check Projects put them to work on something real, with a team.

## bcp
eyebrow: Blank Check Projects
title: Blank check. (Because we're broke.)
photo: bcp-kit

Every fall, new members split into small teams and get four weeks to design and build a themed, interactive display that combines mechanical motion, embedded electronics, programming and promotion.

<!-- "- When | What happens" -->
## bcp.steps
- Week 1 | Get the theme and your kit, meet your technical lead, write your own timeline.
- Weeks 2–3 | Design, print, wire and code. Send a progress summary to your technical lead every week.
- Week 4 | Finish it, then present the display and hand in a short documentation report.

<!-- Source: "AVI Robotics - Blank Check Project Rules & Guidelines". -->
## bcp.spec
- Length: Four weeks
- Brief: A themed, interactive display
- Must combine: Mechanical motion, embedded electronics, programming, promotion
- Each team: Writes its own timeline and has a technical lead
- At the end: A presentation and a documentation report
