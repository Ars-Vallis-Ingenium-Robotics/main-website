## what
eyebrow: What we do
title: Build things. Learn by doing. Give back.

AVI started with underwater robots, but it's here for any member who wants to make something real: robots, projects, workshops and outreach.

<!-- Cards. "wide: yes" makes a card span two columns. -->
### Robots and teams
photo: worlds-display
wide: yes
link: [Our teams](/teams)

Our teams compete and build year-round. Sequoia Marine Solutions builds underwater robots for MATE ROV, and a Micromouse team is forming.

### Blank Check Projects
photo: bcp-build
link: [How it works](/projects)

Four weeks, a small team and a theme: design and build an interactive display with moving parts, electronics and code. Blank check, because we're broke.

### Workshops
photo: workshop-code
link: [The tracks](/projects)

Friday nights in EE 389: programming, electronics, CAD and design, taught by members who learned it the same way.

### Outreach
photo: pi-day-group
wide: yes
link: [Where we've been](#outreach-title)

MESA events, runs, regional competitions and open houses. Robots are a great way to start a conversation about engineering.

## projects
eyebrow: Projects & workshops
title: No experience? Good. Start here.
button: [Projects & workshops](/projects)

Friday workshops teach programming, electronics and CAD from zero. Then every fall, new members team up for a Blank Check Project: four weeks to build an interactive display that moves, lights up and runs code.

## outreach
eyebrow: Outreach
title: Out in the Valley.
