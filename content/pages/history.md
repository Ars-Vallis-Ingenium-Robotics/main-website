## founders
eyebrow: The founders
title: Started by three people in 2025.

[Robert Voss](person:robert-voss), [Declan Doss](person:declan-doss) and [Timothy Cromwell](person:timothy-cromwell) first competed in the [MATE ROV Competition](mate) with Engineering Renaissance's [Crush Depth](crush-depth) team at Clovis Community College. In September 2025 they started AVI at Fresno State.
