# Privacy page (/privacy). Keep this in step with the code: if a feature starts storing something new, say so here.

## hero
kicker: Privacy
title: The short version.

No ads or tracking pixels. The site keeps limited first-party counts for its tools and printed QR codes; we never sell information about you.

## list
### Cookies
We use three small cookies, and only when they're needed. None of them track you, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

- **avi_cart**: remembers what's in your cart. Set only after you add something; gone after 14 days or when you check out.
- **Form protection**: a security token that stops other sites from submitting our forms in your name.
- **avi_admin**: keeps officers, group leads and members signed in to the admin pages. You'll never get it as a visitor. (While signing in with Google, a second short-lived cookie remembers the sign-in attempt for up to 10 minutes.)

### Stored in your browser
The QR code maker runs entirely in your browser. It saves nothing unless you ask: tick "Remember my look" or press "Save look" and your settings stay in this browser only (never sent to us). Untick the box or delete a saved look to clear it. The same goes for dark mode: the site follows your device's setting, and only remembers a choice (in this browser) if you press the theme button.

### Counting tool use
So we know which tools are worth building more of, the tools pages count how often they're opened and which download buttons get pressed, per day. That's the whole record: "QR maker, PNG download, November 16". No cookies, no address, nothing about who you are or what your code says.

### Admin accounts
People an officer adds to the admin pages sign in with Google. We keep their email, name, access level, group and when they last signed in, and nothing from their Google account beyond that. Removing someone deletes their account here.

### Member profiles and hours
Members can fill in a profile (photo, pronouns, links, a short bio). Nothing in it appears on the public site unless they turn on "Show my profile" and an officer or their lead approves it; turning it off takes it down right away. Photos are cropped in the member's browser and only the cropped square is uploaded. Hours members log are visible to them and the leads of their teams, and to everyone in their organization only as a leaderboard total if that organization turns leaderboards on.

### Hours
Members who log hours have their sessions stored: team, category, start and end times, and any note they add.
Their own leads (and the club's officers, for AVI teams) can see and fix them, and download them for sponsor reports
and award letters. If an organization turns on its leaderboard, everyone in that organization sees members' names and
season totals.

### Buying or donating
To hand over a shirt or send a receipt we keep your name, email, optional phone number and your order. Payment happens on PayPal; we never see your card. Officers use this only for your order, and you can ask us to delete it once it's done.

### Videos
Videos come from YouTube, but nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. The preview picture is fetched from YouTube's image server without telling it which page you're on. After you press play, [YouTube's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies (we use their privacy-enhanced mode).

### Our QR codes
When you scan one of our printed codes, we record the day and hour and a broad device category from your browser's user agent. In production, the code's name and full browser user agent are also sent to the officers' Google Sheet. The scan record and sheet do not include your IP address or precise location; the web server's temporary request logs may include the IP address, as explained below.

### Server logs
Like every website, our server briefly logs requests (address, page, time) to keep the site running and safe. We don't use them for anything else.

### Questions
Email [contact@avirobotics.org](mailto:contact@avirobotics.org) to ask what we have about you or to have it deleted.
