# Privacy page (/privacy). Keep this in step with the code: if a feature starts storing something new, say so here.

## hero
kicker: Privacy
title: The short version.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels. We only keep what the site needs to work, and we never sell anything about you.

## list
### Cookies
We use three small cookies, and only when they're needed. None of them track you, so there's no cookie banner to click through.

- **avi_cart**: remembers what's in your cart. Set only after you add something; gone after 14 days or when you check out.
- **Form protection**: a security token that stops other sites from submitting our forms in your name.
- **avi_admin**: keeps officers and team leads signed in to the admin pages. You'll never get it as a visitor.

### Stored in your browser
The QR code maker runs entirely in your browser. It saves nothing unless you ask: tick "Remember my look" or press "Save look" and your settings stay in this browser only (never sent to us). Untick the box or delete a saved look to clear it.

### Counting tool use
So we know which tools are worth building more of, the tools pages count how often they're opened and which download buttons get pressed, per day. That's the whole record: "QR maker, PNG download, November 16". No cookies, no address, nothing about who you are or what your code says.

### Buying or donating
To hand over a shirt or send a receipt we keep your name, email, optional phone number and your order. Payment happens on PayPal; we never see your card. Officers use this only for your order, and you can ask us to delete it once it's done.

### Videos
Videos come from YouTube, but nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. The preview picture is fetched from YouTube's image server without telling it which page you're on. After you press play, [YouTube's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies (we use their privacy-enhanced mode).

### Our QR codes
When you scan one of our printed codes we count the scan: the day and hour, and whether the phone is an iPhone, Android or computer. We also send the code's name and your browser's description (its "user agent") to a Google Sheet the officers use. No location, no IP address, nothing that identifies you.

### Server logs
Like every website, our server briefly logs requests (address, page, time) to keep the site running and safe. We don't use them for anything else.

### Questions
Email [contact@avirobotics.org](mailto:contact@avirobotics.org) to ask what we have about you or to have it deleted.
