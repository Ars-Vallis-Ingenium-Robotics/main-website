# About page (/about)

## hero
kicker: About
title: The Valley of Artistic Ingenuity

*Ars Vallis Ingenium* is Latin for the art, or craft, of the Valley's ingenuity. We're students from STEAM, the humanities and the social sciences building industry-level solutions together.

## purpose
eyebrow: Our purpose
title: Skills you don't get in a classroom.

From our constitution: AVI exists to provide a space where students can develop hands-on and professional skills, and to find opportunities that enrich their academic and professional lives.

Underwater robotics is where we started, but AVI is here to support whatever our members want to build, robotics or otherwise. Teams compete, projects teach, and outreach brings it all back to the Central Valley.

## bcp
eyebrow: Blank Check Projects
title: Blank check. (Because we're broke.)
photo: bcp-build
button: [Projects and workshops](/projects)
note: Run in fall 2025 (Oct 10 to Nov 7) and fall 2026 (Oct 16 to Nov 13).

Every fall, new members split into teams and get four weeks to design and build a themed, interactive display that combines mechanical motion, embedded electronics, programming and promotion.

## bcp.spec
- Length: Four weeks
- Brief: A themed, interactive display
- Must combine: Mechanical motion, embedded electronics, programming, promotion
- Each team: Writes its own timeline and has a technical lead
- Every week: A progress summary to the technical lead
- At the end: A presentation and a documentation report

## majors
eyebrow: Every major
title: You and your talents have value here.
chart-title: Club majors, fall 2025

Our competitions are scored like real engineering companies: the robot, technical documents, presentations, marketing and safety. Our teams take engineers and programmers, and they also take writers, designers, business majors and people who keep 51 students organized.

## majors.departments
- Design & manufacturing: CAD, 3D printing, machining, chassis, manipulators
- Programming & electronics: Control software, GUI, PCBs, power, sensors
- Marketing & logistics: Branding, social, sponsorships, travel, finance
- Safety: Checklists, JSEA, lab policy, deck management
