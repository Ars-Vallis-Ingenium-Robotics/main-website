## Where it started
when: Before AVI

AVI's founders first competed in MATE ROV with Engineering Renaissance's Crush Depth team at Clovis Community College. [Robert Voss](person:robert-voss), [Declan Doss](person:declan-doss) and [Timothy Cromwell](person:timothy-cromwell) brought that experience to Fresno State.

<!-- Source: Robert (Nov 14, 2026). Check the "when" line. -->
## Getting it off the ground
when: Spring – Summer 2025

[Adam Garabedian](person:adam-garabedian) helped us get started fast. He coordinated with our two faculty advisors, [Dr. Alaeddin Bani Milhim](person:alaeddin-bani-milhim) and [Dr. Shuo Wu](person:shuo-wu), and with Fresno State, and made the first connections that let AVI start so quickly.

## AVI Robotics is founded
when: Fall 2025
photo: fall-2025-group

Ars Vallis Ingenium, the Valley of Artistic Ingenuity, starts at Fresno State as a club for every major. The fall goes into training: technical skills, documentation habits, marketing and teamwork.

### Officially a nonprofit (effective date)
date: Sept 2, 2025

The IRS later confirmed AVI's 501(c)(3) status back to this date.

### The company takes shape
date: Sept 2025
photo: fall-2025-leads
caption: The first team leads.

The founders write the first team and subsystem structure for Sequoia Marine Solutions, AVI's MATE ROV company: a C-suite, subsystems and a lead for each.

### The first Blank Check Project
date: Oct 10 – Nov 7, 2025

Teams get four weeks to design and build a themed, interactive display with moving parts, electronics and code. (Blank check, because we're broke.)

### IRS letter arrives
date: Dec 2025
photo: dec-meeting
caption: The last general meeting of the semester.
