About AVI

Ars Vallis Ingenium Robotics.

AVI Robotics is the public-facing name for Ars Vallis Ingenium Robotics, a Fresno and Central Valley nonprofit built around student engineering. The name carries the mission: Ars means art or skill, Vallis means valley, and Ingenium means ingenuity or talent.

Identity

A practical home for student engineering.

AVI exists so students can learn by building real systems under real constraints. The work includes mechanical design, electronics, software, documentation, sponsor communication, fundraising, public speaking, and volunteer coordination.

The organization is intentionally broader than one team. MATE ROV is a major pathway, but AVI’s purpose is to grow a durable robotics culture around Fresno State and the Central Valley.

Fresno State and the Valley

Built around local opportunity.

AVI connects students with the engineering culture around Fresno State and the Central Valley. The point is to make serious robotics feel reachable here: not only for coastal programs, not only for large universities, but for Valley students willing to build.
