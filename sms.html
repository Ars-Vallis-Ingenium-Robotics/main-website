Sequoia Marine Solutions

A marine robotics team with its own deck.

Sequoia Marine Solutions, or SMS, is AVI Robotics’ MATE ROV competition identity: ocean-minded, operations-heavy, and built around the discipline of fielding an underwater system under pressure.

Role inside AVI

One focused team, one larger organization.

SMS handles the MATE ROV challenge work: robot development, mission practice, technical documentation, product presentation, and operations under competition conditions.

AVI provides the wider nonprofit structure: sponsorship, outreach, volunteering, student leadership, and Central Valley robotics education.
