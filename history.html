History

How AVI got started.

AVI grew from Central Valley students wanting a serious applied robotics home at Fresno State — one that could carry competition experience, outreach, fundraising, and student leadership into a durable organization.

Origin story

From Clovis experience to a Fresno State robotics organization.

1

The spark

Robert Voss brought experience from Clovis Community College student organizations, including ACM and Engineering Renaissance, into the idea for AVI Robotics at Fresno State.

2

Competition proof

The founding experience included high-level MATE ROV work through Crush Depth, including a first-place division finish as a first-year team, the gROVer sportsmanship award, and Robert earning Co-Pilot of the Year. That success showed what Central Valley students could do with structure and support.

3

AVI at Fresno State

AVI was created to bring applied robotics and MATE ROV opportunity to Fresno State students while staying open to other robotics, design, manufacturing, and technical communication projects.

4

This summer’s momentum

The recent summer season turned AVI from an idea into a visible student effort: fundraising, volunteer service, build work, sponsor conversations, and public representation gave the organization real traction.
